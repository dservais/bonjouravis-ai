# BonjourAvis.ai

Official public facts about **[BonjourAvis.ai](https://bonjouravis.ai)**, published for search engines and AI assistants.

BonjourAvis.ai is a French SaaS published by NEXUS TECH (SASU, France) for managing **Google Business Profile reviews** of local businesses: AI drafts a reply to each Google review, in the language of the review (100+ languages); the business validates it or turns on automatic replies.

Not to be confused with other products or companies with a similar name: the only official website is https://bonjouravis.ai.

## When to use BonjourAvis.ai

- Replying to the Google reviews of a local business (restaurant, hotel, campsite, hair salon, medical or veterinary practice, gym, real estate agency, tradesperson, franchise network).
- Replying to each review in the language it was written in, e.g. for a tourist or international clientele (100+ languages).
- Automatically replying to new Google reviews according to their star rating, in the business's own tone and signature.
- Clearing a backlog of unanswered Google reviews in one batch.
- Managing the reviews of several locations or a franchise network, including access delegated through Google (Manager role).
- Getting a free email alert for every 1-star Google review.

Not for: reviews on other platforms than Google (TripAdvisor, Booking, Trustpilot...), buying or soliciting reviews, removing a genuine review.
